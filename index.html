Accessible Room Experience — Prototype Walkthrough

4-page guest journey demonstrating every functional requirement in spec.md, built from the production "Plan your stay", "Room Availability", and "Checkout" page structures.

Walk through the journey in order

  1. 1. Plan Your Stay — search widget with Accessible Room toggle + tooltip, and the global Large Font Mode toggle.
  2. 2. Select Your Room & Rate — Room Availability page: always-visible Accessible Room filter, facility icons, Room Details modal, empty-state and SynXis-outage demos.
  3. 3. Confirm Your Reservation — checkout carrying the accessibility designation and facility tags through to booking.
  4. 4. Booking Confirmation — on-screen confirmation plus a confirmation-email preview with reminders and frontline contact info.

Tip: use the journey in order — selections made on pages 1–2 carry forward to 3–4 via the browser session (matching the "persist for the browsing session" decision in spec.md).
